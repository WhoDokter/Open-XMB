Original vs procedural assets

Left: the textures and tables extracted from the PS3 firmware (read from the local assets/ folder, never bundled). Right: the procedural replacement, computed here from a few parameters. Differences are shown amplified.

Each texture (64 x 32, RGB) is fitted per channel with a 2-D polynomial in (u, v) by least squares. Errors are in 8-bit levels: RMS over all pixels and the worst single pixel. Under about 1 RMS / 3 max is hard to see.
